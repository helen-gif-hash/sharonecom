Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

What we collect

SharonEcom is a static website with free tools and blog content. We do not require you to create an account or log in to use any of our tools.

The only personal information we collect is what you voluntarily provide through our contact form — your name, email address, and message content. This information is sent to our email via Web3Forms and is used solely to respond to your inquiry.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages and tools are most used. Google Analytics sets cookies on your browser to collect anonymous usage data (page views, session duration, approximate location). We do not use this data to identify individual users.

You can block these cookies through your browser settings without affecting the functionality of our tools.

Third-party services

  • Google Analytics — anonymous traffic analytics
  • Web3Forms — contact form delivery to our email
  • Cloudflare — website hosting and API functions

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they process data. We do not sell or share your data with any other third parties.

Amazon affiliate links

As an Amazon Associate, we may earn from qualifying purchases made through affiliate links on our site. This does not affect the price you pay. See our Affiliate Disclosure for details.

Your rights

If you have submitted your email through our contact form and would like it removed from our records, email us at contact@sharonecom.com and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the date at the top of this page. We recommend reviewing this page periodically for any changes.
