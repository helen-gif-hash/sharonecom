How the math works
Unit volumeL × W × H → cubic feet (US: inches ÷ 1,728) or cubic metres (cm ÷ 1,000,000; UK: cm ÷ 28,316.8 = cu ft)
Daily sales paceMonthly sales ÷ 30.4
Monthly storage feeUnits × volume × rate — Jan–Sep off-peak, Oct–Dec peak, per marketplace and size tier (rates embedded per market)
Aged surchargeSnapshot on the 15th of each month, per unit of volume by age. US & CA start at 181 days; UK & EU start at 241 days; JP starts at 271 days. The 271–300 day tier is several times the 241–270 tier in every marketplace.
Reorder pointDaily pace × (lead time + safety buffer)
Restock quantityDaily pace × cycle — each PO covers one replenishment cycle; the reorder point bridges the lead-time gap
AssumptionsFIFO depletion (oldest units sell first), restocks arrive on schedule, fees accrue daily, surcharge snapshots monthly on the 15th. Excludes the storage utilization surcharge. The pre-271-day tiers don't apply to Clothing, Shoes, Bags, Jewelry & Watches in most marketplaces.

Fee data: Amazon Seller Central published rate cards (2025–2026), all 12 marketplaces. Poland uses the harmonised EU € rate card; Sweden's aged surcharge is converted from the EU € rate card at Amazon's own SEK storage-fee ratio — confirm in Seller Central. Estimates for planning only.
