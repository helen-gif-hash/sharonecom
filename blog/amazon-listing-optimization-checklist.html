Amazon Listing Optimization Checklist 2026 — Title, Bullets, Keywords That Actually Convert

Sharon Amazon Operations Aug 20, 2026 9 min read

On July 27, 2026, Amazon cut the product title limit from 200 to 75 characters and moved the remaining space into a new "Item highlights" field. If your listings still run 180-character keyword dumps, you are already behind. This checklist covers what moves conversion under the new rules: the 75-character title, converting bullets, keyword placement, images, and A+ content.

Amazon Product Title Requirements 2026: The 75-Character Rule

Effective July 27, 2026, the Item name (what used to be the title) is capped at 75 characters including spaces across all non-media categories and all stores except Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, and the UAE.

To replace the lost space, Amazon introduced a new Item highlights field capped at 125 characters. Your total allowance is still 200 characters — it is just split 75 / 125 instead of one long title. Item highlights appear directly beneath the Item name on both desktop and mobile as of August 10, 2026, and they are searchable — so you do not lose keyword coverage by moving details out of the title.

The AI takeover is already running. Since July 27, 2026, Amazon has been pushing AI-generated title recommendations to non-compliant listings. Brand owners get a 14-day window to review, edit, or reject them in "Review Listing Changes" before they go live. If you do nothing, Amazon rewrites your title for you — and it may not pick the keywords you care about.

Here is what a compliant 2026 title restructure looks like:

Field Before (old style) After (2026 compliant)
Item name (75) Premium Stainless Steel Insulated Water Bottle 32oz Leakproof BPA Free Gym Travel Sports Wide Mouth HydroPeak 32oz Insulated Water Bottle
Item highlights (125) — Leakproof, BPA-free, double-wall stainless steel. Keeps cold 24h / hot 12h. For gym, travel, sports.

The principle: keep the brand, product type, and the single most important attribute in the 75-character Item name. Move materials, use cases, and secondary attributes into Item highlights. Do not try to cram everything back into the title — that is exactly what the new rule punishes.

Amazon Bullet Points That Convert in 2026

Five bullets is still the sweet spot. Amazon indexes bullet content for search, but buyers skim — so each bullet has to earn its place. The common failure is writing bullets as a spec sheet. Specs belong in the description and A+; bullets should answer "why should I buy this?"

  • Lead with the benefit, not the feature. "Stays cold for 24 hours" beats "Double-wall vacuum insulation." The feature is the proof; the benefit is the hook.
  • Put the keyword early, naturally. If "insulated water bottle" is your target term, work it into bullet one without sounding robotic. Amazon reads the first ~200 characters of each bullet most heavily.
  • Keep each bullet under 250 characters. Longer bullets get truncated on mobile and dilute scanability. Our listing grader flags any bullet over that limit.
  • One idea per bullet. Five focused points convert better than three rambling paragraphs.
  • Cover the objections. Size, compatibility, what is in the box, warranty. Buyers who cannot find the answer leave.

Where Amazon Actually Reads Your Keywords in 2026

Keyword placement is not about repeating a term ten times. Amazon's index pulls from several fields, and over-stuffing one of them triggers suppression risk. Here is the priority order:

  1. Item name (75 chars) — highest weight. Your primary keyword must be here.
  2. Item highlights (125 chars) — new in 2026, fully searchable. Use it for secondary keywords and variants.
  3. Bullets and product description — strong supporting weight, written for humans first.
  4. A+ content — indexed but lower weight; great for conversion, not a primary keyword play.
  5. Backend Search Terms — the hidden field. Do not repeat words already in your title or bullets, do not use competitor brand names, and use singular or plural (not both). You get a limited character budget — spend it on synonyms and misspellings real customers use.

Pro tip: Stop pasting competitor ASINs and brand names into Search Terms. Amazon explicitly discourages it, and it can trigger a compliance flag. Use that space for genuine alternate phrasings instead.

Images and A+ Content: Where the Sale Closes

By the time a shopper reaches your bullets, they have already judged your main image. The 2026 bar is high:

  • Main image on pure white, filling 85% of the frame, no text or props. Non-negotiable.
  • Infographic images (2–4) that show dimensions, use cases, and differentiators without needing to read a word.
  • Lifestyle image proving the product in context — this is what builds desire.
  • A+ content for brand-registered sellers: comparison charts and story modules lift conversion 3–10% on mature listings. If you are not brand-registered, that alone is worth the application.

The Complete 2026 Listing Optimization Checklist

Before you publish or refresh a listing, run down this list:

Element 2026 Pass Criteria
Item name ≤ 75 characters, includes brand + product type + top attribute
Item highlights ≤ 125 characters, secondary keywords + use cases, no filler
Bullets 5 bullets, benefit-led, each < 250 characters, primary keyword in #1
Backend Search Terms No repeats, no competitor brands, singular/plural once
Main image White background, 85% frame fill, no text
A+ content Comparison chart + at least one story module (brand-registered)
Mobile check Title + first bullet fully visible without tapping "see more"

The Bottom Line

The 2026 title change is not a suggestion — Amazon is already rewriting non-compliant listings with AI, and you only get a 14-day window to push back. The sellers who win this year are the ones who moved their keyword strategy into the new Item name / Item highlights split early, wrote bullets for humans instead of crawlers, and treated images as the real conversion lever. Optimization is no longer a one-time setup; it is a quarterly refresh. Grade your listing against the 2026 checklist above before your next replenishment cycle and you will catch problems while they are still cheap to fix.
